## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS with no build step, published on GitHub Pages. Settled by the brief ("лёгкий сайт-визитка", "выложим на GitHub"); not asked as a separate question.

## Users

Primary (confirmed): partners and organizations who could help scale «Юрта знаний» into new regions and languages — education authorities, kindergartens and schools as institutions, universities, cultural and social projects, companies, foreign counterparts met at forums and business missions. They usually arrive from a link or QR code Elena shares, often on a phone. Their job: understand in seconds what the project is, check that it is serious, see what already exists, and contact Elena.

Secondary: buyers (institutions, parents) who want a specific game. They are served by the catalogue with prices and links to the product pages on alenmart.ru, not by the main story.

## Product Purpose

A one-page "postcard" site for the educational project «Юрта знаний» (Bashkir: «Белем тирмәһе») by Elena Vladimirovna Nagimova: a series of board and didactic games for learning the Bashkir language (and other languages) and the culture of the native land through play. It complements the existing shop alenmart.ru, which is a large general catalogue of kindergarten equipment where the project gets lost. Success: a partner leaves understanding the mission, trusting it, and writes or calls Elena.

## Positioning

Games are developed by ООО «АленМарт» with Akmulla Bashkir State Pedagogical University (an innovation platform of its Institute of Pedagogy), consultants from the National Museum of the Republic of Bashkortostan and the National Library named after Akhmet-Zaki Validi, philologists and illustrators. The methodology was approved by the Educational-Methodological Council at the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Bashkortostan (protocol No. 6 of 29.11.2023, 22 votes for, none against). Games exist in Russian, Bashkir, Tatar and English; work is under way with Kazakh, Belarusian and Uzbek. The format is built to be repeated for any region and people. Motto: «Играя — изучай свой край!»

## Product Principles

1. Proof before pitch: the Ministry approval, the university platform and the real letters carry credibility, so the documents themselves are shown.
2. Every section leads to one conversation with Elena.
3. Culture is the content: language, ornament, costume and places are the material of the page, not decoration on top of it.
4. Light and durable: static, fast on a phone, editable by hand, with data kept in one place.
