# Surface brief — index.html (landing «Юрта знаний»)

Scope: the single-page "postcard" site, published on GitHub Pages. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

- Audience: partners and organizations (education authorities, schools and kindergartens as institutions, universities, cultural and social projects, companies, forum counterparts in Kazakhstan, Belarus, Uzbekistan). Mostly phone, from Elena's link or QR.
- Job: understand the project in seconds, trust it, see what exists, contact Elena.
- Action: write to Elena (WhatsApp / phone / email). Secondary: buy a game on alenmart.ru.
- Proof: Ministry council approval (protocol 29.11.2023), Akmulla University platform, museum/library consultants, all gratitude letters and awards as real scans, the 18-item catalogue with real prices.
- Constraints: static, no backend; Elena's contacts only; prices from alenmart.ru (October 2026 snapshot); every font must render Ә Ө Ү Ҡ Ғ Ҫ Ҙ Һ Ң.
- Memorable moment: the giant «Семья» card that turns through languages, ending on an empty «+ ваш язык» card that is the partnership ask.
- Unresolved: original high-resolution photos of Elena and the forums (the booklet copies are ~200 px); repository name and domain.

## Direction contract

THESIS: «Юрта знаний» is one didactic card on which a word lives in many languages; the page proves the format carries over to any people and language. It refuses the category's default kids'-product landing (icon-benefit grids, cartoon confetti, testimonial slider).

OWN-WORLD: White card stock on a deep box-frame green field (#0E7A41), titles in box red (#E2231A), alenmart yellow (#FFBB10) reserved for the "+ ваш язык" slot and the primary action, ink #16201A. Podkova (primer slab, 600–800) for card words and headings, Golos Text for reading and UI. Everything sits on cards with 22px radius and a thin inner keyline; language index tabs РУС · БАШ · ТАТ · ENG · ЎЗБ · ҚАЗ · БЕЛ · + are the navigation grammar; the Bashkir letters are the only ornament-scale type.

STORY: The partner understands a series of 18 games and aids teaching Bashkir, Tatar and English and the culture of the land; believes it because the Ministry council approved it, Akmulla University tests it and ministries, colleges and schools thanked it in writing; acts by writing to Elena to bring the format to their region or language, or buys a game on alenmart.ru.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed green. Left half: the motto «Играя — изучай свой край» as H1 in white Podkova (~5.5rem), one sentence on what it is, one proof line, yellow "Стать партнёром" and a ghost "Смотреть игры". Right half: a giant white card (~500×620, tilted −2.5°) with language tabs on its top edge, «СЕМЬЯ» in red at ~7rem cycling to Ғаилә · Гаилә · Family · Oila · Отбасы · Сям'я, the logo children and yurt at the card foot, language label below the word; the last tab "+" turns the card into a dashed yellow slot «Ваш язык?».

FORM: «Одно слово — семь языков» (the trilingual didactic card), #1 on the ordered list of seven, chosen by the user as Impeccable's pick over the rolled #4 «Открой коробку»; seed key 4d08562e. Code-led (no image generation on this machine). Signature interaction: the language tabs turn the hero card (one 3D card flip) and the same tabs filter the catalogue. Motion grammar: cards are dealt once into place (short rise and rotation settle, exponential ease-out), letters lift on hover; reduced motion removes auto-cycling and flips.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
